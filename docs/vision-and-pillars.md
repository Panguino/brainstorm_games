# Vision and pillars

**Status:** Draft  
**Owners:** Both

## One-line pitch

**Race to the center of the galaxy**—pilot through **sector nodes** from the **outer rim** inward, alone or with others; **co-op**, **PvP**, or a **mix**, with the chance to **cross paths** with other players on the way. Flight shifts between **open 3D** and **on-rails** setpieces depending on the sector. *(Draft—tighten tone once pillars are locked.)*

## Player fantasy

<!-- e.g. ace pilot on impossible runs, squad leader, lone wolf, etc. -->

- **Navigator / racer:** chart a path through a **changing galactic map** toward a **final convergence**.
- **Social friction:** sometimes **allies**, sometimes **rivals**—shared highway, contested shortcuts.
- **Learner:** optional **fixed** monuments and **looping** narrative frames reward **knowledge** over brute retries (see [world-and-narrative.md](./world-and-narrative.md)).
- **Dual pilot:** **open 3D** dogfight and navigation where it fits; **on-rails** **cinematic** runs through tunnels and authored paths when we want **Star Fox** readability ([combat-and-flight.md](./combat-and-flight.md)).

**Emerging pillar candidates (from Chris — not ranked yet)**

- Lore and cast **deepen over many runs** (reward the roguelike loop narratively).
- **Run-only power** vs **permanent unlocks** as a clear contract with the player.
- **Build expression:** customization and systems depth that can go “unfair” in the fun way—without ruining baseline challenge (tuning TBD).

**Emerging pillar candidates (from Brad — not ranked yet)**

- **Systems-first depth:** growable **mods / materia-like** items with long-term build paths.
- **High replay surface:** **procedural** routes and content where it does not fight clarity.
- **Stylized tech art:** **simple geometry, loud VFX/lighting** so combat reads at speed.
- **Learnable landmarks:** **fixed** puzzles / monuments players can **master**, dropped into a **changing** map (Rust-style placement lottery).

## Reference touchstones

<!-- Games, films, music—not as a checklist to copy, but to align taste. -->

| Reference | What we want to borrow |
|-----------|-------------------------|
| [Avorion](https://www.avorion.net/) (galaxy map mental model) | **2D sector map**, rim → core progression, sense of **long journey** across discrete locations |
| Star Fox | On-rails readability, vehicle fantasy, setpiece bosses |
| Rust | Learnable **monuments** in a **procgen** placement shell (already in design docs) |
| [World of Warcraft](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/) (raids) | **Multi-phase** bosses—**mechanic literacy** and **puzzle beats**, not tab-target combat copy |
