# World and narrative

**Status:** Draft  
**Owners:** Both

## Setting summary

**Premise (Brad + team — draft)**

- The galaxy is structured as a **2D sector map** (think [Avorion](https://www.avorion.net/)–style **overworld**: dots or regions connected by links). The player begins on the **outer rim** and **races inward** toward the **center**.
- Each **node** on the map resolves to a **mission type** (combat, survival, puzzle, resource, etc.—see [core-loop-and-modes.md](./core-loop-and-modes.md)).
- **Multiplayer posture:** playable **solo**, **co-op**, **versus**, or a **mix**—you can **run into other people** en route (exact networking and session rules TBD in [technical-and-scope.md](./technical-and-scope.md)).

**Story motors (brainstorm — pick primary + B-plots)**

## Replay framing (why keep playing?)

**Ideas on the table — not mutually exclusive**

| Frame | Player-facing pitch | Design notes |
|-------|---------------------|--------------|
| **Time loop** | Each run adds **knowledge** (monuments, enemy tells, map logic); die → retain insight in fiction or mechanics | Aligns with Rust-style **learnable** landmarks |
| **Simulation / training** | Runs are **drills**; a **canonical “real” attempt** exists as meta (e.g. reset = “the real run”) | Clarifies why failure is cheap; needs careful **stakes** so center still matters |
| **Hidden simulation** | Same as above but **do not tell** the player upfront—reveal later | High **trust** risk: can feel brilliant or unfair; if we try it, plan **earnest payoff** and avoid gaslighting |
